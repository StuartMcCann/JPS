About Us

At James Pollock & Son, we’ve been supporting growers, landscapers, and horticultural professionals across the North of Ireland for the last six decades. Our reputation is built on reliability, quality products, and service you can count on. We supply Garden Centres, Nurseries, Landscapers, Hardware Stores, Farm Supply Stores, Councils, Sports Clubs and Growers. With our dedicated delivery fleet and trusted network of partners, we ensure your orders arrive quickly and efficiently- whether you’re nearby or further afield.

  • Fungicides
